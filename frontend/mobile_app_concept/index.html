AI Agronomic Explanation

Why did this happen now?

Unseasonal showers followed by high humidity (72%) provided the 8 hours of leaf wetness required for Bipolaris fungal conidia to penetrate stomata.

What is at risk if delayed?

If left untreated, spores will splash upward to developing panicles during heading, causing chaffy grain and up to 35% harvest loss.

Agronomic Root Cause

Dense tillering foliage restricted aeration near the soil surface. Sandy loam soil with lower potassium buffer allows faster lesion expansion.

Action Plan

Immediate Cultural Action

Drain & Potash Application

Drain standing water to 2-3 cm. Apply 25 kg MOP (Muriate of Potash) per acre to harden epidermal walls.

Biological / Organic Option

Pseudomonas Fluorescens Spray

Foliar spray of Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 2.5 ml/L during cool evening hours.

CIB&RC Approved Chemical

Mancozeb 75% WP

Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L. Always observe 14-day pre-harvest interval.
