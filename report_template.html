3. 📈 Multi-Day Crop Health Recovery Timeline

14-Day Chronological Plot Rehabilitation Protocol (#plants & AI Detect) Crop Lifecycle

Actionable, day-by-day agronomic trajectory guiding farmers from initial disease diagnosis to complete vegetative recovery.

Day 1: Sanitation

Isolate diseased foliage; burn/bury infected plant debris. Switch off overhead sprinklers to prevent spore spread.

Day 3: Spray Action

Apply targeted organic/copper spray (Mancozeb 2.5g/L or Neem Oil 1500ppm) between 06:30–08:30 AM calm window.

Day 7: Lesion Arrest

Inspect halo margins. Active yellow margins turn to dormant brown rings. Apply foliar potassium booster.

Day 14: Clearance

Emergence of lesion-free new apical shoots. Retake AgriShield AI leaf scan to confirm healthy status.

Supported for fungal blight, bacterial blight, yellowing nutrient chlorosis, and healthy prophylactic maintenance.
